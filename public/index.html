Money tools Pactsmith builds for you

The agent starts from fixed templates, so you decide the terms and it handles the contract.

Escrow: hold funds until you approve

  • Payer deposits an exact amount once
  • Only the payer releases it to the payee
  • Anyone can refund the payer after the deadline

Payroll Vault: pay a team on a schedule

  • Fixed payees, amount and number of periods
  • Payees pull their own pay
  • Funded once with the exact total, so every paycheck is guaranteed

Revenue Split: share every payment

  • Shares fixed in basis points, totalling 10,000
  • Recipients withdraw their own share
  • No owner, admin or setter
